ANSWER:
Leiden-based knowledge graph clustering is a method used in the context of knowledge graph construction that focuses on community detection within the graph. The Leiden algorithm is employed to identify top-level communities and create hierarchical subclustering, reaching a depth of 3. This approach allows for a more organized structure within the knowledge graph, as it groups related information together based on the detected communities [Passage 2][Passage 3].

In contrast, normal knowledge graphs may not utilize such advanced community detection techniques. They typically represent relationships between entities without necessarily organizing them into hierarchical structures or communities. The use of the Leiden algorithm in Leiden-based clustering enhances the ability to summarize and retrieve information effectively by structuring the data in a way that reflects its inherent relationships [Passage 1].
