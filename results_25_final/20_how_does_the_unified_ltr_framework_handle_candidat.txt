ANSWER:
The unified learning-to-rank (LTR) framework handles candidates from multiple heterogeneous retrieval channels by integrating them into a single re-ranking model. This approach captures query-dependent channel utility and cross-channel interactions while operating under strict production latency constraints. The framework is designed to jointly optimize multiple business objectives, including clicks, add-to-carts, and purchases, while also incorporating channel-specific objectives and recent user behavioral signals to improve conversion rates in multi-channel ranking [Passage 3][Passage 6][Passage 7].

Additionally, the framework addresses the challenges posed by the independent optimization of channels, which can generate candidates with varying score distributions and bias characteristics. By treating channel information as a signal within the unified ranking framework, it dynamically adjusts to short-term intent shifts that influence channel utility and conversion likelihood [Passage 4][Passage 7].

Overall, the unified LTR framework effectively merges candidates from diverse retrieval channels into a cohesive ranked list, optimizing for user satisfaction and business goals [Passage 5][Passage 8].
