ANSWER:
SuperLocalMemory's adaptive re-ranking layer improves NDCG@5 from 0.441 to 0.900 through the implementation of a rule-based re-ranking system that utilizes over 20 feedback signals. This adaptive learning layer enhances the ranking of search results without degrading the topic-level accuracy, as indicated by the maintained Mean Reciprocal Rank (MRR) of 0.90. The improvement in NDCG@5 represents a 104% increase, while the latency overhead added by this layer is only 20 milliseconds [Passage 1][Passage 3]. 

The documents do not provide specific details on the nature of the feedback signals or the exact mechanisms of the re-ranking process beyond stating that it involves behavioral analysis and re-ranking [Passage 6].
